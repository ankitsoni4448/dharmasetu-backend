All 89 discovery records point to SanskritDocuments pages. Its published FAQ allows personal study and teaching but restricts copying or reposting for website promotion or commercial use without permission. Those links are therefore references for human research only; they do not establish publication rights, a chosen edition, exact Sanskrit text, or independent review.

Each discovery candidate remains blocked on human Sanskrit text review, edition-level provenance, transliteration review, and rights clearance. Meaning, practice, pronunciation, and audio require separate sources and separate verification. The numerical difference between 29 and 108 is 79, but 108 additional eligible records are required to reach a catalog of 108 verified records while the existing 29 remain unverified.

Machine-readable findings are in `data/mantra/m4/source_research_report.json` and `data/mantra/m4/publication_blockers.json`. The publication gate rejects records without `rights_status` and `rights_reference` even if other approval fields are present.
